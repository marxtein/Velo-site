Join Us / 加入我们

一起，算出一条路来

聚变不该只属于巨型装置和漫长试错。我们以物理原生算法为轴心，以开源软件为基石，以高性能计算为引擎——让聚变研发，更快，更开放，更可协作。

Fusion shouldn't belong only to massive facilities and decades of trial and error. With physics-native algorithms as our axis, open-source software as our foundation, and high-performance computing as our engine — we accelerate fusion R&D to be faster, more open, and more collaborative.
